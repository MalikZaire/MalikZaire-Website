About

Malik Zaire Martin is an Atlanta-based model and creative with Chicago roots. At 6′4″ with an athletic build shaped by years as a college athlete and fitness professional, he brings a presence that reads just as well in a tailored editorial as it does in a gym campaign.

He's building MalikZaire as a clean, professional personal brand — separate work, separate standard. The focus: editorial, commercial and e-commerce, fitness, underwear & swim, runway and events, and acting and on-camera work.

Off set, Malik is a content creator working to turn creative work into a business that can employ other people too. On set, that same mindset shows up simple: early, prepared, and easy to direct.

Shooting info / FAQ
What should I include in a booking inquiry?

Your name or brand, the type of shoot, the date or date range, the location, how long you need him, and your budget or rate. The booking form below walks you through all of it — the more detail up front, the faster the reply.

What kind of work does Malik accept?

Editorial and fashion, commercial and e-commerce, fitness and athletic campaigns, commercial underwear and swim, runway, showroom and fittings, live brand events, and acting, commercial and UGC on-camera work.

Does he travel?

Yes. Atlanta is home base and Chicago is a second base, so both cities book easily. Travel further out is open for paid assignments — put the location and dates in your inquiry.

What about digitals?

What's on this page is the current portfolio. A fresh digitals session is also being lined up; ask in your inquiry and I'll tell you honestly where the new set stands.

What about rates?

Rates depend on the type of work, usage and time involved. Send the project details and budget in your inquiry and you'll get a straight answer back — no games either way.
